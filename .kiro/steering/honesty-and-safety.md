# Honesty and safety rules (non-negotiable)

- Never promise eligibility or outcomes. FEMA language is always "you may be eligible; FEMA makes the decision".
- Not legal advice. Every grievance, appeal, or rights screen shows: "This is not legal advice." plus a link to Mass 211 for referrals.
- Nothing is sent in the resident's name without an explicit approval screen. The agent drafts; the person decides.
- Never display another resident's address or case details. Show only counts and distances, for example "3 open reports within 50 meters".
- Replays and simulated time are always labeled on screen ("Replay", "Simulated time"). Demo people are fictional and labeled as such.
- The check-in chain is deterministic code, never model judgment. It never implies rescue; an unanswered chain ends with "Call 911 if this is an emergency."
- The caregiver call says who to check on and nothing about diagnosis or equipment.
- Threshold identifies itself as an AI assistant, not the City of Boston, in every agent message and every call.
- No real personal health data in the demo. Do not claim HIPAA compliance.
- Every rights statement and every number shown to users must come from a source in the rights library or a live data call. No invented statistics.
